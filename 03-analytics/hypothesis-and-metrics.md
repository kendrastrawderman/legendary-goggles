## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** Users leave the app because content curation is poor.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** 11% of users stay on the app for longer than 30 minutes.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** A long-time streaming subscriber who wants to discover great new content but feels overwhelmed by the size of the catalog. They want to find something worth watching quickly without spending excessive time browsing. They end up leaving the app because content curation is poor.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Users don't have to think about finding content. Personalized, quality recommendations are front and center.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Better recommendations will stop wanderers from wandering. They will stay on the app longer, moving them out of the Wanderer category and into the Casual Brower or Power User categories, which have higher LTVs.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** A higher percentage of users stay on the app for longer than 30 minutes.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Casual Browsers and Power Users must not decrease.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** 1 or 2 months?
- **Draft your full hypothesis sentence , one to three sentences; quote the metric, name the persona, name the outcome:** Most users leave the app after 30 minutes because content curation is poor. I believe that improving content curation  will result in a 15% decrease in Wanderers. I will protect Casual Browers and Power Users from decreasing and will make a go/no-go decision after 60 days.
